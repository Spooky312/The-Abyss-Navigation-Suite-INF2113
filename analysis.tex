\section*{Executive Summary}

This report documents the systematic application of Brigman Deep Sea Technology's proprietary BCIC (Breakdown, Clarify, Interpret, Categorize) methodology to analyze Triton Deepwater's Project Specification for the Abyss Navigator Suite. As Triton's selected vendor, we transformed ambiguous requirements into actionable, testable specifications ready for SRS development.

Key outcomes of this analysis phase include:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Identification and resolution of 15 incongruities (26.3\% rate) preventing costly implementation rework
    \item Transformation of 57 ambiguous statements into 79 measurable requirements (22 new atomic requirements created)
    \item Establishment of quantitative specifications for safety, communication, and operational parameters
    \item Development of categorized requirements framework with 100\% traceability to PS clauses
    \item Significant strengthening of customer relationship through structured professional engagement
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Phase Status:} Analysis activities are 100\% complete. The project is ready to transition to SRS development.

\section{Interpret Activity: Requirement Transformation}

\subsection{Objective}
To transform clarified stakeholder requirements into precise, testable statements compliant with industry standards.

\subsection{What Happened}
Each requirement was reinterpreted using IEEE 830 standards, with our deep-sea operational expertise ensuring technical feasibility while maintaining alignment with stakeholder intent.

\subsubsection*{Interpreted Requirement: SYS-SAF-001}
\textbf{Original (BO-L1-4):} \textit{"The business requires appropriate handling of abnormal or emergency conditions during missions."}

\textbf{Interpreted:} \textit{"The system shall detect abnormal conditions exceeding predefined operational parameters within 5 seconds of onset. Upon detection, the system shall initiate Level 1 emergency protocols within 10 seconds, transitioning to safe operational states while maintaining alignment with PS Section 6.4 safety requirements."}

\subsubsection*{Interpreted Requirement: COM-REQ-001}
\textbf{Original (IT-L0-C2):} \textit{"IT solutions shall operate under conditions of constrained bandwidth, latency, and intermittent connectivity."}

\textbf{Interpreted:} \textit{"The communication subsystem shall maintain mission-critical functionality under bandwidth constraints of 1kbps with maximum latency of 30 seconds. The system shall implement robust mechanisms to handle intermittent connectivity exceeding 60 seconds duration without data loss."}

\subsubsection*{Interpreted Requirement: DATA-ACC-001}
\textbf{Original (6.6):} \textit{"We require accountability for mission activities and operational decisions."}

\textbf{Interpreted:} \textit{"All mission activities, operational decisions, and system state changes shall be logged with millisecond timestamps, operator identification, and contextual metadata. Logs shall be retained for 7 years in tamper-evident, secure storage supporting regulatory compliance and post-mission investigation."}

\subsection{What Went Right}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Quantitative Transformation:} Subjective statements became measurable requirements
    \item \textbf{Technical Feasibility:} Operational expertise ensured realistic requirement definition
    \item \textbf{Traceability Maintenance:} Bidirectional links to original requirements preserved
    \item \textbf{Standard Compliance:} All requirements aligned with industry verification standards
\end{itemize}

\subsection{What Went Wrong}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Initial Over-Specification:} Early interpretations included unnecessary implementation details
    \item \textbf{Validation Bottlenecks:} Technical feasibility reviews created temporary workflow delays
    \item \textbf{Terminology Alignment:} Required multiple iterations to achieve consistent terminology
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Findings}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Created 22 new atomic requirements to address implicit stakeholder needs
    \item Established quantitative parameters for 100\% of safety-critical requirements
    \item Developed standardized requirement templates for future project reuse
    \item Achieved 100\% testability for all interpreted requirements
\end{itemize}

\section{Risk Analysis and Mitigation}

\subsection{Critical Technical Risks Addressed}

\subsubsection{Risk: Communication Specification Ambiguity}
\textbf{Description:} PS communication constraints lacked quantitative specifications.\\
\textbf{Impact:} System may fail under actual deep-sea communication conditions (Severity: High).\\
\textbf{Likelihood:} Probable ($\geq$60\% occurrence).\\
\textbf{Mitigation:} Established quantitative bandwidth (1kbps) and latency (30s) definitions through stakeholder validation.

\subsubsection{Risk: Emergency Protocol Definition Gap}
\textbf{Description:} Emergency handling requirements lacked operational procedures.\\
\textbf{Impact:} Inconsistent emergency response could compromise mission safety (Severity: Critical).\\
\textbf{Likelihood:} Possible (40-60\% occurrence).\\
\textbf{Mitigation:} Developed detailed emergency response protocols with quantitative thresholds.

\subsubsection{Risk: Sensor Integration Complexity}
\textbf{Description:} Sensor integration requirements lacked interface specifications.\\
\textbf{Impact:} Integration delays affecting project schedule (Severity: High).\\
\textbf{Likelihood:} Probable ($\geq$60\% occurrence).\\
\textbf{Mitigation:} Early prototyping approach and Interface Control Document development.

\subsubsection{Risk: Autonomy Level Misalignment}
\textbf{Description:} Unmanned operations lacked clear autonomy level definitions.\\
\textbf{Impact:} System may not properly balance autonomous operation (Severity: Medium).\\
\textbf{Likelihood:} Possible (40-60\% occurrence).\\
\textbf{Mitigation:} Defined autonomy levels and handoff protocols through scenario analysis.

\subsection{Project Management Risks Managed}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Scope Creep:} Controlled through formal change management and stakeholder alignment
    \item \textbf{Stakeholder Availability:} Managed via structured engagement schedule and escalation protocols

\end{itemize}
