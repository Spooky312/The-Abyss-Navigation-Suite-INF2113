\section{Purpose of This Document}
This document records the formal requirements analysis performed by Brigman Deep Sea Technology on the Project Specification (PS) for the Abyss Navigator Suite. Following the BCIC (Breakdown, Clarify, Interpret, Categorize) methodology, this analysis identifies and resolves incongruities, clarifies ambiguities, and prepares requirements for subsequent Software Requirements Specification (SRS) development. This report represents the output of the Analysing method within the Requirements Engineering framework.

\section{Executive Summary}
This report presents the findings from Brigman Deep Sea Technology's analysis of the Project Specification (PS) for the \textbf{Abyss Navigator Suite}. Our systematic BCIC approach has identified critical gaps and ambiguities that require clarification to ensure successful implementation of the unmanned, autonomous deep-sea submersible control system.

Key analysis outcomes include:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Communication Specification Gap:} Quantitative definitions for bandwidth, latency, and intermittent connectivity thresholds are absent.
    \item \textbf{Safety Protocol Deficiency:} Emergency handling lacks operational procedures, escalation protocols, and defined threshold values.
    \item \textbf{Interface Definition Shortfall:} Sensor integration requirements omit interface protocols, data formats, and power specifications.
    \item \textbf{Autonomy Level Ambiguity:} Unmanned operations context requires clearer definition of autonomy levels and remote intervention conditions.
    \item \textbf{Data Management Specificity:} Traceability requirements need concrete data retention periods and format standards.
\end{itemize}

Through structured stakeholder engagement and rigorous analysis, we have transmuted ambiguous requirements into testable statements ready for SRS integration.

\subsection{Interpret Activity}
\label{subsec:interpret}

Based on clarification outcomes, we reinterpreted ambiguous PS requirements into precise, testable statements compliant with IEEE 830 standards.

\subsubsection*{Original Requirement: BO-L1-4 (Emergency Handling)}
\textit{"The business requires appropriate handling of abnormal or emergency conditions during missions."}

\subsubsection*{Interpreted Requirement: SYS-SAF-001}
\textit{"The system shall detect abnormal conditions exceeding predefined operational parameters within 5 seconds of onset. Upon detection, the system shall initiate Level 1 emergency protocols within 10 seconds, transitioning to safe operational states while maintaining alignment with PS Section 6.4 safety requirements."}

\subsubsection*{Original Requirement: IT-L0-C2 (Communication Constraint)}
\textit{"IT solutions shall operate under conditions of constrained bandwidth, latency, and intermittent connectivity."}

\subsubsection*{Interpreted Requirement: COM-REQ-001}
\textit{"The communication subsystem shall maintain mission-critical functionality under bandwidth constraints of 1kbps with maximum latency of 30 seconds. The system shall implement store-and-forward protocols and adaptive compression algorithms to handle intermittent connectivity exceeding 60 seconds duration."}

\subsubsection*{Original Requirement: 6.6 (Operational Accountability)}
\textit{"We require accountability for mission activities and operational decisions."}

\subsubsection*{Interpreted Requirement: DATA-ACC-001}
\textit{"All mission activities, operational decisions, and system state changes shall be logged with millisecond timestamps, operator identification, and contextual metadata. Logs shall be retained for 7 years in WORM storage with cryptographic integrity verification, supporting regulatory compliance and post-mission investigation."}

\section{Risk Analysis and Mitigation}
\label{sec:risks}

Our analysis identifies several implementation risks requiring proactive mitigation.

\subsection{Critical Technical Risks}

\subsubsection{Risk: Communication Specification Ambiguity}
\textbf{Description:} PS communication constraints lack quantitative specifications, creating implementation uncertainty.\\
\textbf{Impact:} System may fail under actual deep-sea communication conditions (Severity: High).\\
\textbf{Likelihood:} Probable ($\geq$60\% occurrence).\\
\textbf{Mitigation Strategy:} Establish quantitative communication requirements through prototype testing and stakeholder validation.

\subsubsection{Risk: Emergency Protocol Definition Gap}
\textbf{Description:} Emergency handling requirements lack operational procedures and escalation protocols.\\
\textbf{Impact:} Inconsistent emergency response could compromise mission safety (Severity: Critical).\\
\textbf{Likelihood:} Possible (40-60\% occurrence).\\
\textbf{Mitigation Strategy:} Joint development of detailed emergency response protocols with Triton operational staff.

\subsubsection{Risk: Sensor Integration Complexity}
\textbf{Description:} Sensor integration requirements lack interface specifications and protocol definitions.\\
\textbf{Impact:} Integration delays affecting project schedule and functionality (Severity: High).\\
\textbf{Likelihood:} Probable ($\geq$60\% occurrence).\\
\textbf{Mitigation Strategy:} Early prototyping of sensor interfaces and development of Interface Control Documents (ICDs).

\subsubsection{Risk: Autonomy Level Misalignment}
\textbf{Description:} Unmanned operations context lacks clear autonomy level definitions.\\
\textbf{Impact:} System may not properly balance autonomous operation with necessary human oversight (Severity: Medium).\\
\textbf{Likelihood:} Possible (40-60\% occurrence).\\
\textbf{Mitigation Strategy:} Definition of autonomy levels and handoff protocols through operational scenario analysis.

\subsection{Project Management Risks}

\subsubsection{Risk: Scope Creep Post-Clarification}
\textbf{Description:} Stakeholder clarification may introduce requirements beyond original PS scope.\\
\textbf{Impact:} Budget and schedule overruns affecting project viability.\\
\textbf{Mitigation Strategy:} Formal change control process managed through Account Manager escalation.

\subsubsection{Risk: Stakeholder Knowledge Attenuation}
\textbf{Description:} Key stakeholder availability may decrease during implementation phase.\\
\textbf{Impact:} Loss of contextual knowledge affecting requirement interpretation.\\
\textbf{Mitigation Strategy:} Comprehensive documentation and knowledge transfer protocols.
