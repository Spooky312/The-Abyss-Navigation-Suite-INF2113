\noindent\fbox{\begin{minipage}{\dimexpr\textwidth-2\fboxsep-2\fboxrule\relax}
\small
\textbf{Purpose.} This form is issued by Paragon Assurance to the QC team (Veridion Systems Validation Labs) as part of the QA oversight of the Requirements Engineering (RE) process audit conducted on the Biometric Student Attendance System project.

Paragon Assurance is required to verify that the QC team produced and retained the requisite process artefacts in connection with its audit activities. The QC team is required to confirm whether each listed artefact was produced and can be produced for review, or to declare that the artefact is non-retrievable.

This declaration is required to formally close the QA review of the QC process and maintain the integrity of the overall audit record.

\medskip
\textbf{Instructions.} For each artefact listed:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,nosep,topsep=2pt]
\item Tick \textbf{Retrievable} if the artefact was produced and can be submitted for review.
\item Tick \textbf{Non-retrievable} if the artefact was not produced or cannot be located.
\item If \textbf{Non-retrievable}, provide a written explanation in the \textbf{Declaration} section at the end of this form.
\end{itemize}
\end{minipage}}
