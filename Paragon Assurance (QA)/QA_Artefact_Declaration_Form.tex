% PURPOSE

\noindent\fbox{\begin{minipage}{\dimexpr\textwidth-2\fboxsep-2\fboxrule\relax}
\small
\textbf{Purpose.} This form is issued by Paragon Assurance as part of the QA audit of the Requirements Engineering (RE) process conducted by Nevon Solutions for the Biometric Student Attendance System project, commissioned by BioSec Educational Institution (Customer P1-BIOSEC).

The QA audit identified artefacts that were expected to be present in the project repository but were absent or non-compliant at the time of inspection. Nevon Solutions is required to either upload the outstanding artefacts to the repository and confirm this below, or declare that the artefact is non-retrievable.

This declaration is required to formally close the audit findings and maintain the integrity of the process record.

\medskip
\textbf{Instructions.} For each artefact listed:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,nosep,topsep=2pt]
\item Tick \textbf{Retrievable} if the artefact has been committed to the repository or can be produced for review.
\item Tick \textbf{Non-retrievable} if the artefact cannot be located or no longer exists.
\item If \textbf{Non-retrievable}, provide a written explanation in the \textbf{Declaration} section at the end of this form.
\end{itemize}
\end{minipage}}
