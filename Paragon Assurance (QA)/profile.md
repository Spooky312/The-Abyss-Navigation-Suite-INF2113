## Quality Assurance Provider

### Company Name
**Paragon Assurance**

### Role
Independent **Quality Assurance (QA)** authority responsible for auditing the software development process and reviewing requirements artefacts for completeness, consistency, and standards compliance.

### Company Overview
Paragon Assurance is an independent quality assurance consultancy specialising in process audits and requirements-phase reviews for software development projects. The organisation provides structured QA audits to ensure that development teams adhere to defined processes, that requirements artefacts meet quality standards, and that identified deficiencies are documented and tracked for resolution.

### Responsibilities

- Audit the software development process for adherence to defined standards.
- Review requirements artefacts for completeness, correctness, and consistency.
- Identify and document non-conformances and deficiencies.
- Track and verify the resolution of raised issues.
- Provide independent QA audit reports to project stakeholders.
- Ensure the requirements baseline is fit for entry into the development phase.
